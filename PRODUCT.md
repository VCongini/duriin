# Duriin Product Brief

## Product register

Duriin is a personal creator brand and gaming-first media site. It is a brand surface rather than a workflow-heavy application: the interface should communicate personality, make current activity legible, and move visitors into relevant video content quickly.

## Primary audience and purpose

The primary audience is viewers discovering or returning to Duriin's gaming clips. Builds and coding experiments are useful secondary content, but they must not compete with video discovery. A visitor should understand what Duriin is, find recent or archived videos, and start watching with minimal effort.

## Brand personality

The brand should feel energetic, creator-led, and technically confident. It should retain personality without leaning on generic esports neon, glass-heavy gaming UI, interchangeable SaaS cards, or other synthetic "AI-designed" conventions.

## Theme system

Brutalist and Modern are intentional alternate presentations of the same brand and should remain available alongside light and dark color modes. Theme controls are utilities, not primary content, so they should be compact and visually quiet.

- Brutalist should emphasize raw editorial structure, explicit borders, direct typography, and purposeful asymmetry. It should not become visually broken, illegible, or parody-brutalist.
- Modern should emphasize restrained contemporary polish, softer depth, and clean hierarchy. It should not become a generic glassmorphism dashboard.
- Both themes must preserve the same content hierarchy, interaction semantics, and recognizable Duriin character.

## Experience priorities

1. Gaming video discovery and playback.
2. Clear current activity, featured content, and archive browsing.
3. Builds, coding, and supporting creator context.
4. Theme customization as a quiet preference.

## Accessibility and quality bar

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across both themes, both color modes, mobile and desktop layouts, keyboard and screen-reader use, visible focus, reduced motion, contrast, and touch target sizing. Progressive enhancement is preferred: core navigation, content, and outbound playback must remain understandable when media embeds or remote feeds fail.
